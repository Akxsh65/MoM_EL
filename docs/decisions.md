- 2026-11-27: T5 TDMS defaults checked against Gulati et al., ISMIR 2016 (read the paper PDF directly): paper = 120 bins (10 cents/bin), delay tau = 0.3 s (explored 0.2-1.5 s; invariant except >=1 s on CMD), power compression alpha = 0.75 applied before smoothing, 2-D circular Gaussian smoothing sigma = 2 bins (explored 1/2/3; kernel truncated at 4 sigma each side), L1 normalisation of the final surface, unvoiced frames excluded, tonic-normalised cents, octave-wrapped, kNN with KL/Bhattacharyya distances (86.7% on CMD, whole recordings, leave-one-out). OUR differences: windowed (30 s) surfaces, 24/40/60 bins instead of 120 (30 s windows have only ~3000 pairs, 120x120 = 14400 cells would be too sparse), sigma = 1 bin (about as wide in cents as the paper's at 40 bins), logistic regression instead of kNN, pairs kept only when BOTH frames are voiced, mean-1 global rescale of features (no per-feature standardisation). Kernel truncation and circular wrap match the paper.
